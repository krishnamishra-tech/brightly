Questions

Things people ask before they sign

How much roof space do I need?

As a rule of thumb, about 100 square feet per kilowatt. A typical 3 kW home system needs around 300 square feet of clear, unshaded roof.

Will it work during a power cut?

A standard grid-tied system switches off during a grid outage for safety reasons. If you want backup, we can add a battery-based hybrid setup. We will explain the cost difference at the survey.

What is net metering?

Your DISCOM installs a bidirectional meter. Power you do not use goes to the grid and is credited against your bill. We prepare and file the application for you.

How much maintenance does solar need?

Very little. Panels should be rinsed every few weeks, more often in dusty areas. Our care plan covers cleaning and yearly inverter checks.

Is there a government subsidy?

Subsidy schemes for residential rooftop solar change from time to time. We check what you are eligible for and show it separately in your quote.
