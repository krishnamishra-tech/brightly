Sunvale Solar was started to make going solar feel less confusing and less risky.

Our story

Why we started

Too many solar quotes were vague about what was actually going on the roof. We decided to put everything in writing: the panel brand, the inverter model, the wiring, the warranty and the expected output.

Today we are a team of installers, designers and paperwork specialists working across Pune and Mumbai. We take on fewer projects at a time so each one gets a proper survey and a clean install.

How we work

Three promises

Quote in writing

Every component is named in your quote. If something changes, you hear about it before it happens.

Tidy installs

Neat cable runs, labelled isolators and a clean site when we leave. Our crews are trained, not hired by the day.

We stay reachable

One number, answered by a person. If output drops, we look at it quickly.
